# Problem 7: Target and Email Reflection

## Customer target ranking

### 1. Restaura Hospitality Group

Restaura is my top-ranked target. The company builds high-end dining facilities for senior living
complexes, and I do not think I would have found this company on my own. AI targeted this company
well. The facilities it will build are large, and it uses higher-end building materials. This kind
of company will almost certainly build more facilities in the future, which could create excellent
ongoing business.

### 2. Titan Hospitality Group

Titan Hospitality is my second-ranked target. It is an eleven-restaurant and catering-hall
management group in Maryland that appears to operate fairly high-end, large venues. The company
will probably continue adding to its portfolio. Its projects would also be high-profile with the
public and could potentially lead to future business from competitors that observe the work.

### 3. Glendale Dining Services

Glendale Dining Services is also an appropriate target. As a large dining-services company, it
could produce repeat business. It has a broad portfolio that includes senior living, residential
facilities, and schools. Although it may be a good customer, its website makes the company appear
more budget-conscious. Nursing-home dining services are also less visually exciting than the
opportunities represented by the two higher-ranked targets.

## Outreach email reflection

### Restaura Hospitality Group

The Restaura email was very good at capturing the higher-end culinary and technology highlights
that go into the company's planning. The body seems highly targeted to Restaura. This draft could
be used as written.

### Glendale Dining Services

The AI took a different but effective approach for Glendale. It focused both on Singer Equipment's
kitchen-construction capabilities and on Singer's smallwares—such as glasses and plates—and supply
chain for restaurant supplies. This draft could also be used as written.

### Titan Hospitality Group

The Titan email was the most vague and sounded the most AI-generated. The phrase “your restaurant
portfolio ... caught my attention” is generic. This may be partly because Titan's website
communicates the quality of its high-end restaurants more through photographs than through text.
The email would need to be rewritten with details that are more specific to Titan before it could
be used.

### Overall assessment

The Restaura and Glendale drafts are sufficiently targeted to stand as written. The Titan draft
needs more specific personalization and less generic language.
